# DemandAI: changes from the 29–30 Oct review

For Shantanu. A short summary of what you asked for (the change-request PDF and our two calls on 30 Oct), what is done, and what is still open. Branch `remediation/poc-hardening`, not committed or pushed yet.

## Done

**Branding**
- The Client's look (blue `#004F9B`, navy navbar, yellow emblem, logo on the navbar and sign-in, favicon, email buttons, Word exports).
- It is switched on with one setting, `APP_THEME` (set to the Client's theme folder). The logo files stay out of Git, because the repository is public and never names the client.

**Setup is now editable (by an Admin)**
- **ICP and rubric:**
  - Change the weights and level points, remove parts, and add new parts. A new part reads a lead-file column (country, revenue, site capacity, and so on) with "value → points" rules.
  - Also editable: the fit floor, tier cut-offs, size bands, vertical levels (and new verticals by keyword), personas, the knock-outs and region, and how much and how long each signal counts.
  - Weights must add up to 100%.
  - "Preview tier changes" shows how many accounts of the newest scored list would move tier before you save.
  - Every save is a new config version, with what changed in words and why. Lists already scored keep their version, and Analytics splits G2 by version.
  - A lock for the measurement weeks: locked, or "needs the sponsor's sign-off".
- **Actions:** add, edit and retire actions: name, what it means and when to use it, the signals it suits, an optional segment. The acceptance rate is shown for each action.
- **Signals:** adding a signal type no longer needs a micro-segment, only a sentence on what it means. The AI uses that sentence.
- **Micro-segments list:** every segment the AI or a person created, with its theme, status, and how many accounts and lists use it. A manager can approve, rename, merge or retire one; merging moves its items.
- **Measurement and proceed criteria:** moved here from Analytics. This includes the deal-size threshold, which is now editable.
- **AI service panel:** collapsible, with a one-line summary.

**AI chooses the micro-segment and the next best action** (PDF sections A, B, C and G; each is off until switched on)
- **Segments:** ten accounts per call. The AI reuses existing segments before inventing new ones. A segment still forms only with 3 or more accounts.
- **New segments:** they wait for a manager's approval, and their items wait in For Review tagged "New segment: needs approval". Approving the segment releases the items that waited only for it.
- **Actions:**
  - Each action comes with a runner-up, a reason citing the signals, the claims it would use, and a confidence.
  - The action library guides the choice, and the team's recent decisions (approvals, R6 rejections, edits, outcomes) are sent with every call.
  - An action outside the library goes to For Review tagged "New action: not in the action library".
- **Checks:** every AI answer must cite real signals of its own account and only approved claims.
- **Why the AI chose it:** For Review and the Micro-segments & NBA panel show the segment (new or not, its theme, why, confidence) and the action (in the library or not, why, the signals it cited with type, date and detail, the claims it would use, confidence, runner-up).
- **Changing the segment:** in For Review, a reviewer can move an item to another micro-segment, saying why. The reason is logged for the learning loop, and the message is redrafted for the new segment. "Use this instead" and "Write your own action" also take a reason now.
- **Labels:** the Micro-segments tiles say "Created by AI" (and "Waits for approval" until a manager approves it), "Grouped by AI" when the AI reused an existing segment, or "Created by a person". After a rename or merge, older builds show their items under the new name.
- **Fallback:** when the AI is off or fails, today's rules run, so a build never stops. The build screen says how many segments and actions came from the AI.
- **No library action:** when a segment has no library action, the rules use a generic "Follow up on the …".

**Content library: the AI reads any guideline** (section K, and your points 4–7)
- **Formats:** PDF, Word, PowerPoint, .txt and .md, including rules written in prose.
- **What it suggests:** the rules, writing guidance (a new "Voice and tone notes" field), plays per situation (angle, objection, answer) and frameworks (objective, steps, ask).
- **Every suggestion shows the sentence it came from;** a quote that isn't in the document is refused.
- **A person adds each suggestion to the draft,** and nothing is live until the version is saved.
- **The old reader stays as the fallback** when the AI is off, and it now also works on PDF, Word and PowerPoint.
- **The message-writing AI now gets:** the frameworks (it follows the steps and ends with the ask), the full play, the competitor list, the writing notes, and approved claims matched by topic instead of segment tag. So it writes for new segments and actions too.
- **The brand and claim checks still run as rules on every message.**

**Analytics** (sections I and J)
- **Order:** the criteria first (read-only, with a link to Setup), the gates next, the success areas last.
- **Area names** now use the platform's names: Prospecting, Account Scoring, Signals, Micro-segments NBA and routing, Messages and content, Business value.
- **Too early to call:** below the minimum, a gate shows only "Too early to call: 3 of 10 decisions", never a percentage.
- **G1 fixed:** review minutes are divided by the items a person handled, not by every released item.
- **(i) explanations** on every metric, in Analytics only (as you asked, not on the Today or For Review tiles). They say what it means, how it's worked out with your own numbers, the target, and an example. The wording is from section J.
- **Learning loop:**
  - The first column is now person · company · micro-segment · tier.
  - "Not decided yet" now says where the item is ("Waiting in For Review", "Went ahead, not rated").
  - A plain explanation line at the top, and an (i) on each flag.
  - A new table compares AI-chosen and rule-chosen actions.
- **Pilot readout** rewritten as a short report: the result, the six areas with verdict and key number, what worked, what didn't, and the decision needed.
- **Weekly summary:** adds new accounts, new prospects and new actions (went ahead, in For Review, released after review), each against last week.
