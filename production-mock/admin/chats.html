Chat flowsWhat each chat runs, step by step, and the rules behind it
Everything in the sales workspace starts in a chat. These are the flows a chat can run, in the order the work usually goes: find, score, segment and route, act, track replies, learn. The steps are the same ones people see in the chat while the AI works. The rules each flow runs within are in Rules, and every action is logged in the Audit log.

1 · Find prospectsAccounts
Ask it
“Find maintenance heads at refineries in Thailand”“Find prospects like my last 10 wins”
Steps the AI runs (shown in the chat as it works)
  1. Turn the request into a filter: role, seniority, place, industry and size
  2. Search the primary contact provider, then the fallback for the misses
  3. Cross-check fallback hits and give everyone an identity tier (1 to 4)
  4. Remove existing customers and accounts another rep owns (CRM, read-only)
  5. Hold tier-4 identities for a person to check
Produces
A list named after the request, open beside the chat. "Save as a list" adds it to Accounts.
Who decides
The AI; held records go to the admin

2 · ScoreAccounts
Ask it
“Score this list”“Why is Quartz Olefins ranked first?”
Steps the AI runs (shown in the chat as it works)
  1. Collect live signals from 23 sources: hiring, news, website visits, tech stack, CRM
  2. Keep a signal only if it is observable, predictive and time-bound
  3. Weigh by tier: 100, 60 or 30 points, then apply decay
  4. Stack into Timing: strongest + ½ second + ¼ third, as a share of 200
  5. Score = Fit × Timing ÷ 100, out of 100; tier A ≥ 40, B ≥ 20
Produces
The scored list, ranked, with the process behind an ⓘ.
Who decides
The AI

3 · Segment and routeSegments, Campaigns
Ask it
“Route to NBA and NBC”“Create micro-segments”“What's close to becoming a campaign?”
Steps the AI runs (shown in the chat as it works)
  1. Group accounts by their strongest trigger into micro-segments
  2. Check each segment against the campaign threshold (10 accounts in 7 days)
  3. 10 or more: one draft campaign (NBC) for the whole segment
  4. Below the threshold: each account gets its own next best action (NBA)
  5. Sensitive segments never become campaigns
Produces
Segments with their mode, a draft campaign and the next actions.
Who decides
The sales manager confirms campaigns

Who decides whatSegments and NBA screen
4 · Next best actions (NBA)Segments, Approvals
Ask it
“Prepare the next actions”“Send them”“What should I do with Cedar?”
Steps the AI runs (shown in the chat as it works)
  1. De-dup: no open opportunity, owner confirmed
  2. Read the CRM history and the warm path
  3. Pick email or LinkedIn and write the draft from the proven message structure
  4. Run the brand, claims and banned-word checks
  5. Run the six checks: high confidence, proven message, not sensitive, identity confirmed, not in another sequence, not picked for a spot-check
  6. Passes all six: goes ahead on its own. Fails one: waits in Approvals with the failed check
Produces
One action per account, tracked from prepared to replied.
Who decides
The AI within the rules; exceptions go to the rep or manager
Settings
Six checksReview queue
5 · Campaigns (NBC)Campaigns, person panel
Ask it
“Build the campaign”“Add a LinkedIn step to the turnaround campaign”“How are the campaigns doing?”
Steps the AI runs (shown in the chat as it works)
  1. Use the message structure closest to the trigger (proven or provisional)
  2. Plan the steps across email and LinkedIn, 3 to 5 days apart
  3. Personalise each step for each person
  4. Check nobody is already in another sequence
  5. The manager confirms; step 1 goes out in the next send window
  6. When someone replies, their remaining steps pause
Produces
A sequence per campaign, editable per person.
Who decides
The sales manager
Settings
Message templatesContent library
6 · Replies and hand-offInbox
Ask it
“Pull the emails we received today”“Which replies are objections?”“Draft a reply to Dian”
Steps the AI runs (shown in the chat as it works)
  1. Collect replies from email and LinkedIn
  2. Classify each one: positive, objection, meeting booked, out of office
  3. Pause the remaining campaign steps for that person
  4. Draft an answer and run the brand checks
  5. A positive reply can go to the proposal team with its full context
Produces
Replies in the chat and in Inbox, each with a drafted answer.
Who decides
The rep sends; the manager can reassign
Settings
Rep mailboxes and LinkedIn
7 · ContentContent studio
Ask it
“Write a blog on planning a control system migration”“Make it shorter”“Add proof”
Steps the AI runs (shown in the chat as it works)
  1. Turn the request into a brief: audience, segment and the angle
  2. Outline, then write in the brand voice
  3. Check the claims against the approved list
  4. Change it by asking, or one paragraph at a time
  5. Save it to the Content library
Produces
A blog or whitepaper. PPT and PDF, brochures, infographics, images, carousels, LinkedIn posts, podcasts and video are coming soon.
Who decides
The person who asked; sign-off for new claims
Settings
Brand voice and claimsContent studio screen
8 · Objectives and catch-upRecent chats, Today
Ask it
“Let's get our red accounts to a next step this week”“Remind me Friday at 9”“Note: call Mei after the review”“Catch me up”
Steps the AI runs (shown in the chat as it works)
  1. Name the chat for the goal
  2. List the accounts most urgent first
  3. Track progress as items get done (by the AI or a person)
  4. Keep reminders and notes with the chat
  5. Open with a catch-up when you come back
Produces
A named chat in Recent chats with progress, reminders and notes.
Who decides
The person who set the goal
9 · AnalyticsAnalytics
Ask it
“How are we doing this month?”“How is Kenji doing?”“Where are we stuck?”“Which campaign is performing best?”
Steps the AI runs (shown in the chat as it works)
  1. Pull the numbers for the period and the rep you name
  2. Compare them with the previous period
  3. Move the dashboard to the same period and rep
  4. End with what to do about it
Produces
An answer in the chat, with the dashboard beside it.
Who decides

10 · Learning loopAnalytics, Admin home
Ask it
“What did we learn this week?”“What worked last month?”
Steps the AI runs (shown in the chat as it works)
  1. Read the outcomes: wins, misses, skips and their reasons
  2. Promote a message structure after 10 outcomes at 60% or better; demote below 60%
  3. Compare signal sources by how often they convert
  4. The next run starts from what changed
Produces
What changed, in the chat and in Analytics. The full run is the monthly learning job.
Who decides
The AI proposes; the admin approves rule changes
