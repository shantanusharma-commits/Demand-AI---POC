The five criteriaAll five required
1
Confidence: high or medium
Confidence: high is 0.75 or more; confidence: medium is 0.50 to 0.75; both pass. Confidence: low (under 0.50) goes to nurture and nobody is asked to decide.
2
Message verification passes
Approved claims or cited signals only, no banned phrase, every figure has a source. An unproven message is not a failure.
3
Every protective check passes
Exclusions, suppression, open deal, a touch in the last 14 days. If a check cannot run, the action is held (fail closed).
4
Identity tier is I1 or I2
I1 Apollo first try; I2 ZoomInfo after Apollo missed and Lusha agrees. I3 and I5 get a card; I4 is held.
5
The segment's policy allows it
Set per segment on the right.
All five met: Ready to send, the rep sends with one click (assisted mode). Any one failed: an approval card to the owning rep, due in 3 days, then it expires and the account re-ranks.
