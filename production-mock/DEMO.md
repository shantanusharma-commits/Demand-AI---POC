# Demand AI demo: click-through

About 15 minutes. Open `index.html` in Chrome, in a private window so every thread opens fresh, and sign in as **Daniel (sales manager)**. For dictation to work, use the hosted link or allow the microphone when Chrome asks.

| # | Click | Talk about |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sign in as Daniel. | Every screen has the same layout: Copilot on the left and the work on the right. The chat opens on the dotted line, *New since you last looked*, with yesterday's exchange above it. |
| 2 | Point at the orange **Red accounts** callout. | A chat that needs you asks for your attention: in the chat, on the board and in Recent chats. |
| 3 | On the board: **Where the team stands**, then **While you were away** (switch Since you last logged in / Today / This week). | Outcomes come first. Every number opens its list, and the markers show who did the work: the AI, a person, or waiting for you. |
| 4 | Scroll to **Your day**. Switch **Day → Week → List**, then filter to **Decide**. | The same items in three shapes: meetings with their prep and the decisions tied to them, the week as a calendar, or one priority list. |
| 5 | Close Copilot with the **×**. The **Copilot** pill floats bottom left; click it to bring the chat back. | When you need the full screen, Copilot steps aside but stays one click away. |
| 6 | In the composer, open **Start something new**. | The quick actions mirror the main menu: prospects, scoring, segments, next actions, campaigns, replies, content, results and objectives. |
| 7 | Click the **mic** and ask "Where are we stuck?" | You can talk instead of type. You see the trend, then the fixes. |
| 8 | Click **Pick up** on Red accounts. Then click the **pen** in the Copilot header and add a note. | An objective opens on its catch-up. Notes live in Copilot and come back with every catch-up. |
| 9 | From Inbox, click **New chat**. Name it, then ask "Find prospects like my last 10 wins". | A new chat starts in the centre, wherever you are. Once the AI has put something together, the list opens beside the chat. |
| 9b | Open a person (for example Sapphire Aromatics) and ask **Pull the last touchpoint**, then **Draft a follow-up**. | The answer is in the chat: what was sent, what happened, what to do next, and the draft. |
| 10 | In **Your day**, click **Open prep** on Cedar renewal strategy. On **Engagement**, click **Skip** and pick a reason. | The person panel opens beside Copilot. A skip asks why, and that reason teaches the next recommendation. |
| 11 | Open **Inbox**, then click **Hand to proposals** on Dian Putri. Close Copilot to get the full mail view. | The inbox has Copilot too, and a positive reply goes to the proposal team with its full context. |
| 12 | Click **Analytics**, then **How much do agents do on their own?** | The dashboard sits beside its chat. Autonomy is a dial that the manager sets. |
| 13 | Click **Switch to Sofia (rep)**. | Sofia's Cedar renewal chat asks for her attention: Mei Tan opened the comparison twice. |
| 14 | Sign in as **Rajiv (admin)**. Walk **Rules** (autonomy ceiling, the six checks), **Users and roles**, **Audit log** and **Settings → Appearance**. | The admin sets the ceiling, and managers set the dial below it. Every action is logged with the rules version it ran under. Admins set the theme the same way as in Proposal AI. |

Close on this: everything starts in one conversation, in the same place on every screen. The agents do the routine work, and people make the decisions.
