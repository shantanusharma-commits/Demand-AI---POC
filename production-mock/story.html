01 · The product

One product. Three questions. A loop that makes the answers better every month.

Sellers stop building lists, chasing signals and writing first drafts. They log in to a day that's already prepared.

1

Who to contact

A clean, identity-checked list built from the customer's own ICP and lookalikes of their wins. Existing customers and owned accounts are removed, and every person carries a confidence tier.

Prospect
2

Why now

Live signals (new leaders, contract renewals, expansion, intent, website visits and more) are qualified, weighted, decayed and stacked. A good fit with nothing happening waits.

Score = Fit × Timing
3

What to say

The next best action for each account: the action, the contact, the channel (email or LinkedIn) and a ready draft, written against message structures that have already worked.

NBA · NBC

The story to tell: the AI does the work, and the salesperson only approves or edits. Anything uncertain goes to a person with the reason attached; sensitive items go to the sales manager to sign off; everything else goes ahead on its own.

03 · The engine

"Why now" is a number you can explain.

Fit and Timing are multiplied, not added. A perfect-fit account with nothing happening ranks zero; one strong signal beats a pile of weak ones.

06 · The learning loop

More accurate every month, without a bigger model.

Misses count as much as wins. Every result is logged against its segment and message structure, and a monthly job decides what to keep.

Pattern promotionA segment + message structure becomes "proven" after 10+ outcomes at ≥ 60% confidence; demoted if the 90-day rate drops.
DecayA proven pattern that isn't re-confirmed fades back to provisional.
Explore / exploitA small share always tries a different structure, so winners keep being re-earned.
Source scoringSources whose signals convert are weighted up; failing ones are flagged for renewal review.
Anomaly detectionA signal far outside its normal range triggers a temporary tier-1 boost and a real-time alert.
ExplainableEvery ranking keeps its evidence and threshold version, so March's decision is still explainable in June.
07 · Battle card

What we sell, why we're different, why they buy.

For any conversation with a prospect or a stakeholder.

What

What we're selling

  • A seller's day, already prepared.Who to contact, why now, what to say, ready when they log in.
  • Approve, edit or ask.A chat-first workspace; no list-building, no signal-chasing, no first drafts.
  • Manager oversight built in.Sensitive and C-level messages come to the manager; team progress at a glance.
  • Runs on the customer's own ICP, CRM and channels.Read-only by default; outreach by email and LinkedIn.
Different

What makes us different

  • Fit × Timing, multiplied.Not a lead score: a perfect-fit account with nothing happening ranks zero.
  • Signals that fade and stack.Qualified, read for strength, decayed and stacked, so one strong signal beats a pile of weak ones.
  • Learns structure, not scripts.Two accounts get the same approach, never the same words.
  • Learns from misses.No-reply counts; coverage gaps and miscalibration are told apart and fixed differently.
  • Data you can trust.Identity tiers and cross-checks; disputed records are held, not guessed.
  • Every decision explainable.Full audit trail with threshold versions.
Why buy

Why a prospect buys

  • Reps spend time selling, not researching.Measured as G1: the share of work that goes ahead without a person.
  • Recommendations reps actually use.Measured as G2: accepted first time.
  • Earlier conversations.Reach the new leader in week one, the renewal six months out.
  • No double outreach.Live-owner checks and send coordination across email and LinkedIn.
  • Gets better on its own.Monthly re-weighting from their own outcomes.

Likely objections

"We already buy contact data."
Good. That's an input. We decide who to contact and when, cross-check the data, and write the action.
"Our reps won't trust AI messages."
They approve or edit everything uncertain, and every recommendation shows why. Trust is measured (G2), not assumed.
"Will it email our customers or spam accounts?"
Closed-won and live opportunities are excluded, and cross-channel conflicts are caught before anything sends.
