Know who to contact, why now, and what to say, with the AI doing the work and the seller deciding.

DemandAI (the full Proposal AI) finds the right accounts from a customer's own ICP, notices the moment they're ready to buy, picks the next best action, writes it, and learns from every outcome. Sellers approve or edit; they don't build lists, chase signals or write first drafts.

Reference point · 27 Oct 2026 · Gathered from the locked Prospect, Score and NBA process flows. No new scope.

1What we're selling

One product, three questions answered in order, and a loop that makes the answers better every month.

Who to contact

A clean, identity-checked list built from the customer's own ICP and lookalikes of their wins. Existing customers and owned accounts are removed, and every person carries a confidence tier.

Why now

Live signals (new leaders, contract renewals, expansion, intent, website visits and more) are qualified, weighted, decayed and stacked. Score = Fit × Timing, so a good fit with nothing happening waits.

What to say

The next best action for each account: the action, the contact, the channel and a ready draft, written against message structures that have already worked. Ten or more similar accounts become a campaign.

The story to tell: the AI does the work, and the salesperson only approves or edits. Anything uncertain goes to a person with the reason attached; everything else goes ahead on its own.

4The learning loop

What makes it more accurate every month without a bigger model. Misses count as much as wins.

Pattern promotionA segment + message structure becomes "proven" after 10+ outcomes at ≥ 60% confidence; demoted if the 90-day rate drops.
DecayA proven pattern that isn't re-confirmed fades back to provisional.
Explore / exploitA small share always tries a different structure, so winners keep being re-earned and better ones can take over.
Source scoringSources whose signals convert are weighted up; failing ones are flagged for a renewal review.
Anomaly detectionA signal far outside its normal range triggers a temporary tier-1 boost and a real-time alert.
ExplainableEvery ranking keeps its evidence and the threshold version in effect, so March's decision is still explainable in June.

5Battle card

For any conversation with a prospect or a stakeholder: what we sell, why we're different, and why they buy.

What What we're selling

  • A seller's day, already prepared. Who to contact, why now, what to say, ready when they log in.
  • Approve, edit or ask. A chat-first workspace; no list-building, no signal-chasing, no first drafts.
  • Manager oversight built in. Sensitive and C-level messages come to the manager; team progress at a glance.
  • It runs on the customer's own ICP, CRM and channels. Read-only by default.

Different What makes us different

  • Fit × Timing, multiplied. Not a lead score: a perfect-fit account with nothing happening ranks zero.
  • Signals that fade and stack. Each signal is qualified, read for strength, decayed, and stacked so one strong signal beats a pile of weak ones.
  • Learns structure, not scripts. Proven message structures, never canned text, so two accounts get the same approach, never the same words.
  • Learns from misses. No-reply counts; coverage gaps and miscalibration are told apart and fixed differently.
  • Data you can trust. Identity tiers and cross-checks; disputed records are held, not guessed.
  • Every decision explainable. Full audit trail with threshold versions.

Why buy Why a prospect buys

  • Reps spend time selling, not researching. Measured as G1: the share of work that goes ahead without a person.
  • Recommendations reps actually use. G2: accepted first time.
  • Earlier conversations. Reach the new leader in week one, the renewal six months out.
  • No double outreach. Live-owner checks and send coordination across channels.
  • Gets better on its own. Monthly re-weighting from their own outcomes.
Likely objections
"We already buy contact data."
Good. That's an input. We decide who to contact and when, cross-check the data, and write the action.
"Our reps won't trust AI messages."
They approve or edit everything uncertain, and every recommendation shows why. Trust is measured (G2), not assumed.
"Will it email our customers or spam accounts?"
Closed-won and live opportunities are excluded, and cross-channel conflicts are caught before anything sends.
