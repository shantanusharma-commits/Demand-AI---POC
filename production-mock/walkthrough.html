Demo walkthrough · about 12 minutes

Tell it as one conversation, not a tour of screens.

Start with what the AI did on its own, then the handoff to the person. Build up one step at a time. Every view is the chat zooming in on the work, and the chat zooming back out.

Chat-ledEverything starts from a question or an instruction.
Anchors on the leftToday, the views and recent chats are bookmarks into the work.
Details on the rightWhatever you open sits beside the chat, never on top of it.
AI or person✦ AI marks what the system did on its own.
Before you start: open the workspace in a fresh tab so the session starts clean. Sign in as Sofia for scenes 1–6. Switch to Pavan from the profile menu (bottom left) for scene 7.
1

What the AI did since I was last here

Today · Sofia
Click and show
  1. Land on Today. Don't touch the chat box yet.
  2. Point at What the AI did since you last logged in: accounts re-checked, segments updated, campaign steps sent, next actions that went ahead, replies, meetings.
  3. Flip the toggle: Since you last logged in → Today → This week.
  4. Point at the ✦ AI marks and the legend.
Say
"Before Sofia does anything, the system has already worked. Overnight it re-checked her accounts, updated the segments, sent campaign steps and the next best actions that passed every check."
"It's like an unread count for her work: since she last logged in, today, or this week."
2

Then it handed these to me

Today · Sofia
Click and show
  1. Scroll to Your numbers; click Emails sent. A chat starts with the list as a tile, open in full on the right.
  2. Back on Today, show Then it handed these to you, most urgent first.
  3. In the chat box, type # and pick Reply to Dian Putri. It opens in its own window: the chat on the left, the reply on the right with a drafted answer.
Say
"Only the exceptions come to a person. Everything here is sorted by urgency, and each one opens in its own window."
Point out: the right pane's header lines up with the chat's; the chat says what it's talking about.
3

Set my objective for the week

New chat · Sofia
Click and show
  1. Click the microphone, or type: I want to work my red accounts this week.
  2. The chat renames itself Red accounts · week of 26 Oct and tracks progress.
  3. Type Note: Budi prefers LinkedIn, then Remind me on Wednesday at 9 to pick up the rest.
  4. Show the Any agent picker: pick the Campaign agent to work on sequences.
Say
"She plans her week in one sentence. The chat becomes the place she comes back to, with her notes and a reminder, so she can pick up exactly where she left off."
4

Zoom in on one account, zoom back out

Red accounts chat · Sofia
Click and show
  1. Expand the red accounts list; click Quartz Olefins. Hui Min Lim opens on the right.
  2. Engagement: the sequence, step by step, each with what it was built from (the signal, the message structure, best practice).
  3. Ask in the chat: Make step 1 shorter, then Why these messages?. The change appears on the right.
  4. Click Send. Back arrow to the list, ✕ to close.
Say
"The chat is her copilot. When an account is open, the conversation is about that account. She never leaves the chat to do the work."
Point out: ✦ Sent on steps the AI sent; a person icon on what she approved.
5

Come back on Wednesday

Recent chats · Sofia
Click and show
  1. In Recent chats, point at Red accounts: 3/5 and a red ! because the reminder is due.
  2. Open it. A catch-up tile: what the AI did meanwhile, what she did, what's left to close the objective.
  3. Click Open the 2 left.
Say
"This is something a general chatbot can't do. Between Monday and Wednesday the system kept working. When she comes back, the thread catches her up and tells her what's left."

The manager's view

Today · Pavan

The line to end on

"Most of the work is done by the AI, on its own, inside the rules you set. People handle the exceptions and the conversations, and everything is one continuous chat they can always pick up from."
