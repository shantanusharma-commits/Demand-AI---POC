#### 3. How they generate their outputs
1. **Data in.** Records are built from licensed directories; the Lusha Community Program, where members share the email headers and signature blocks of their business contacts; data from affiliates and from users' connected Google or Microsoft mailboxes; public-web scanning; and machine-learning auto-completion such as inferring email patterns ([data sources](https://www.lusha.com/data-sources)). Lusha is registered as a California data broker ([data sources](https://www.lusha.com/data-sources)).
2. **Verification and freshness.** Each email carries a confidence score, and each phone and email shows when it was last updated. Each record has 77 fields ([home](https://www.lusha.com), [data](https://www.lusha.com/data)).
3. **Signals.** There are 26 dated signal types in four groups: Growth (headcount and hiring surges, IT spend), Intent (LinkedIn activity, website traffic), Events (job change, promotion, funding, leadership and other news) and Risk (decreases, risk news). 24 are company signals and 2 are contact signals ([buying signals](https://www.lusha.com/buying-signals)). Intent comes from public LinkedIn activity mapped to topics plus changes in website traffic ([buying signals](https://www.lusha.com/buying-signals)).
4. **ICP definition.** In ICP Hub an admin builds Target Profiles: one set of account criteria plus up to four persona sets, with inclusion and exclusion rules. A connected Salesforce or HubSpot removes records already in the CRM ([help centre, search extract](https://info.lusha.com/en/articles/662006-icp-hub-how-ai-recommendations-work)).
5. **Scoring.** Signal Score (0 to 100) is the number of qualifying signals present divided by the maximum available. ICP Fit Score measures the match to the Target Profile. The two combine into a Lead Score that sets the ranking. A record with no signals is shown as "No active signals" rather than 0 ([Knowledge Hub, search extract](https://docs.lusha.com/user-guide/ai-recommendations/signal-score-and-icp-fit-score)). An "Opportunity Conversion Score (Beta)" is listed in the docs, but its method is not published.
6. **Daily feed.** Each morning Home shows up to 10,000 contacts and 10,000 companies, ranked, each with its score and signal. New signals land about twice a week and a full re-ranking runs weekly. Without a profile, the feed is "Lusha generated" from the account's own search and reveal history ([Knowledge Hub, search extract](https://docs.lusha.com/user-guide/ai-recommendations/how-ai-recommendations-work), [Deep Intelligence, search extract](https://lusha.com/organizations?page=60)).
7. **Action out.** The rep reveals a contact (credits are spent only at this step), pulls the buying group and exports to the CRM, a sequence (Lusha Engage) or an automation (Clay, Make, n8n, Zapier). Agents working through MCP can search, enrich and save tables, but "do not send messages or add contacts to sequences" ([buying signals](https://www.lusha.com/buying-signals)).
8. **Human in the loop.** The rep chooses whom to reveal and contact. Lusha ranks the list and explains why; it does not act on its own.

#### 4. Technical mechanisms and interesting approaches
- **Novel: the feed is personalised by usage.** Activity-based recommendations look at up to 90 days or 1,500 reveals and return lookalikes; every reveal trains the model ([help centre, search extract](https://info.lusha.com/en/articles/257536-ai-recommendations)). Deep Intel learns from closed-won patterns and CRM history ([EvoLusha recap](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/what-hundreds-gtm-teams-learned-evolusha-2026-lushadata-vrdqf)). The model type is not published.
- **Novel: data quality is published.** Lusha publishes accuracy per field, refresh cadence per signal, and a decay study: across 148,000 records, 12.25% of US sales leaders changed roles in 12 months (September 2026) ([home](https://www.lusha.com), [buying signals](https://www.lusha.com/buying-signals)).
- **Transparent scoring formula.** Signal Score is a count-based ratio rather than a black box. Every row carries a reason, and that reason becomes the rep's opening line ([Deep Intelligence, search extract](https://lusha.com/organizations?page=60)).
- **One record everywhere.** The same record is served to the extension, MCP, API and CRM: "One database, one refresh cycle" ([home](https://www.lusha.com)).
- **Agent-first distribution.** The CEO argues that "bad data compounds faster through an agent than through a rep", and says Lusha's own AEs have gone "headless" and rarely open Salesforce ([podcast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nhZvmksKoaA)).
- **Stack from jobs:** Kafka and Spark streaming, with BI and data-platform engineering in Tel Aviv ([job posting](https://www.lusha.com/careers/co/tel-aviv/0B.272/senior-bi-data-engineer)). LLM vendor not published. Identity resolution method not published beyond "identity graph" for LinkedIn URLs ([data](https://www.lusha.com/data)).
- **Conflicting plan detail:** one help article says AI Recommendations are on Premium and Scale only ([help centre, search extract](https://info.lusha.com/en/articles/634189-how-ai-recommendations-work)); the Knowledge Hub says all plans ([Knowledge Hub, search extract](https://docs.lusha.com/user-guide/ai-recommendations/how-ai-recommendations-work)).

#### 5. Why they get the results they get
- **Accuracy where it counts:** direct dials and mobiles for phone-first teams (Anaplan, Empiric). Every customer story is about reaching the person, not about AI.
- **Low friction and low entry price:** a free plan and a Chrome extension used inside LinkedIn mean adoption starts with individual reps and spreads bottom-up (VWO, RST Solutions).
- **Proprietary data with community sourcing:** owning the graph lets Lusha publish a refresh cycle and sell the same record to people and to agents. Inference: this is a lasting cost advantage over tools that resell other vendors' data.
- **Defensive repositioning:** by putting its data inside Clay, Claude and ChatGPT, Lusha stays the data layer as interfaces commoditise. This is the CEO's stated thesis ([podcast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nhZvmksKoaA)).

#### 6. What we can learn and adopt
- **Lead scoring:** split our score into two visible parts, Fit (0 to 1) and Signal (count-based), and show "No active signals" separately from missing data. This keeps our tiers explainable to the pilot client's sales team.
- **Lead scoring:** add a "signal age" column and publish our own decay assumptions (for example, a funding signal halves in weight after 90 days), as Lusha does with dated signals and refresh cadences.
- **Next Best Action (NBA) and Next Best Campaign (NBC):** produce a daily "who to call today and why" table per rep, with the triggering signal written as the first line of the suggested message.
- **Qualified lead identification:** add CRM-based exclusion (do not recommend accounts already open in the CRM) and an admin-owned shared ICP profile so new reps start from the same targeting logic.

#### 3. How they generate their outputs
1. **Source.** Bring in accounts and people from Clay's search (company, people and jobs, with no row limits in Audiences), from the CRM (SOQL imports, HubSpot deals), from warehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery), webhooks, inboxes or CSV ([blog index](https://www.clay.com/blog/series-d), [changelog](https://www.clay.com/changelog)).
2. **Enrich through waterfalls.** Each field runs a ranked chain of providers. A free "Infer Email" step runs first; the chain stops at the first confident result; failed lookups are refunded. Emails are validated by ZeroBounce by default, catch-alls are optional ([waterfall guide, search extract](https://www.clay.com/guides/waterfall-enrichment), [Clay University, search extract](https://university.clay.com/lessons/enrich-people-waterfalls-clay-101)). Default provider order comes "from our experience with hundreds of thousands of enrichments" ([Clay University, search extract](https://university.clay.com/lessons/enriching-company-data)).
3. **Research with AI.** Claygent (a web-browsing LLM agent) answers custom questions for each row, such as "does this company build its own agents?" Account Agents keep account intelligence current ([Pendo](https://www.clay.com/customers/pendo), [blog index](https://www.clay.com/blog/series-d)).
4. **Signals.** Job changes, promotions, new hires, web intent (site visitors) and custom signals feed tables and Audiences ([Clay University docs, search extract](https://university.clay.com/docs)).
5. **Score and route.** Customers build their own scoring with formulas and AI columns, then write it back to Salesforce or HubSpot (AlertMedia, Pendo). Clay does not ship a fixed scoring model.
6. **Generate and execute.** AI columns draft copy. Clay Sequencer sends email; Clay Ads syncs audiences to six ad platforms; integrations push to Outreach, Nooks and others ([changelog](https://www.clay.com/changelog)).
7. **Measure.** Reporting in Audiences shows the pipeline and closed-won revenue that came from Clay-sourced accounts ([changelog](https://www.clay.com/changelog)).
8. **Human in the loop:** GTM engineers design and test every play (Sandbox Mode, Function testing). In the stories, BDRs review AI drafts before sending (Pendo) and reps confirm MEDDPICC extractions (AlertMedia).

#### 3. How they generate their outputs
1. **Define who to pursue.** Pull companies and people from the 300M+ database (40M+ companies), Sales Navigator searches, Google Maps, Google search, LinkedIn post engagers ("conversational intent"), hiring signals, event attendees, competitors' customers, a CRM, CSV or webhook ([sales intelligence](https://bitscale.ai/solutions/lp-sales-intelligence), [docs index](https://docs.bitscale.ai/llms.txt), [Pensa](https://bitscale.ai/case-studies/how-pensa-systems-built-scalable-outbound-for-enterprise-cpg-with-bitscale)).
2. **Enrich through waterfalls.** Providers are queried in sequence. Users can reorder them by drag and drop or turn them off, and pay only for the provider that returns a valid result. Optional validation runs inside the waterfall ([waterfall docs](https://docs.bitscale.ai/ingredients/tools-waterfall)). Each field (work email, personal email, phone, LinkedIn URL) has its own chain. Provider order is "optimized by your target segment... from historical accuracy data". Emails pass syntax, MX and SMTP checks, and switchboard numbers are filtered out (vendor claim, [waterfall](https://bitscale.ai/product/waterfall)).
3. **Research and signals.** BitAgent reads the company's website first and then Google, and writes back a structured yes/no or a value with sources. It is tested on 5 rows before scaling, limited by run conditions, and scheduled daily or weekly ([BitAgent](https://bitscale.ai/product/bitagent)). There are 50+ intent enrichments and 15 buying triggers that can be combined, for example Series B plus hiring SDRs plus a new VP Sales ([home](https://bitscale.ai)).
4. **Score and tier.** In the published reference design (GTM Academy SI-6), every signal lands in a "Signal Feed" grid with a signal quality score, an ICP fit score and a combined priority. An LLM prompt classifies each new signal as duplicate, extension or new. A signal-to-persona map picks whom to contact ([SI-6](https://docs.bitscale.ai/academy/signal-intelligence/signal-workflows)).
5. **Route.** Tier 1 (high signal and high fit) is exported automatically to the sequencer; Tier 2 goes to a **manual review queue**; Tier 3 goes to nurture; anything below the threshold is logged ([SI-6](https://docs.bitscale.ai/academy/signal-intelligence/signal-workflows)).
6. **Generate and act.** AI copy references the exact signal ("Your recent Series B suggests ISO 27001 may be on the roadmap", [Scrut](https://bitscale.ai/case-studies/how-scrut-io-built-a-compliance-signal-gtm-machine-with-bitscale)). Contacts are pushed to Instantly, Smartlead, HeyReach, Lemlist, Outreach or Outplay; HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio and Zoho are synced both ways and only changed fields are written back ([home](https://bitscale.ai), [docs index](https://docs.bitscale.ai/llms.txt)).
7. **Measure.** The docs give target metrics: signal-to-outreach above 40%, signal-to-reply above 8% (against 3 to 5% for non-signal outreach), and over 70% of signals acted on within the optimal window ([SI-6](https://docs.bitscale.ai/academy/signal-intelligence/signal-workflows)). These are targets set in teaching material, not measured results.
8. **Human in the loop:** Tier 2 signals and Slack alerts for Tier 1 show the generated email for someone to "review and approve" before export, unless the signal type has been pre-approved ([SI-6](https://docs.bitscale.ai/academy/signal-intelligence/signal-workflows)).
