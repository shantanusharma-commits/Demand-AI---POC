# Why customers like Unify and Lightfield

Research date: 27 October 2026. Purpose: explain WHY customers value these two products (not only their numbers), and what Demand AI (new-logo acquisition, pilot client selling high-ticket industrial products on long cycles) can adopt.

Method and coverage:
- Every story was re-read in full with Tavily extract on 27 October 2026.
- Unify: a Tavily map of https://www.unifygtm.com/customers returned 12 story URLs. The "Meet more of our customers" block on each story page links the remaining 4 (Innovate Energy Group, Peridio, Quo, Anrok), confirming 16 stories in total. All 16 were read.
- Lightfield: a Tavily map of https://lightfield.com/customers returned 7 story URLs. All 7 were read, plus https://lightfield.com/product/ai-pipeline-generation.
- All figures in both sets are customer stories published by the vendor, so every number is a **vendor claim** unless marked otherwise. No third-party verification was found or attempted for this pass.
- "Inference:" marks my own analysis.

### 1.2 Why customers like Unify (themes)

1. **The signal tells them who to contact and when (timing).** 16 of 16 stories run plays off live intent (web, product, hires, CRM, social). Clearest in Perplexity ("talk to prospects at the right time"), IEG ("when they're most likely to convert"), Peridio, Justworks, Spellbook, HyperComply, Navattic, Abacum. Inference: customers value the trigger more than the data itself.
2. **Signal and send in one place (tool consolidation).** 12 stories name the tools replaced or describe stitching: Pylon, Campfire, Spellbook, CandorIQ, Juicebox, HyperComply, Justworks, Navattic, Quo, Together AI, Anrok, Abacum. Justworks explains why it matters: "The signal is the reason why we're reaching out, and we want to reference that in the email we send."
3. **Runs a serious motion without hiring SDRs.** 9 stories: Guru ("without a full-time SDR"), Campfire, CandorIQ, Juicebox, Navattic, Peridio, Together AI, Quo ("infinitely more scalable than managing a large SDR team"), IEG ("the work of a sales and marketing team combined").
4. **Hands-on human help (strategist, professional services, support).** 9 stories: Guru (on-site PS, weekly standups), IEG (strategist "instrumental"), Peridio (Product Growth Strategist), Perplexity (team set goals and recommended plays on day one), Campfire ("best of any vendor"), Anrok ("Support inevitably matters as much as features"), Quo, Navattic, Juicebox.
5. **Fast time to first value.** 9 stories: Abacum (under 2 hours, first play same day), Quo (first play in a day), Justworks (3 plays in 3 days), Together AI (under a week), Navattic (pipeline in 10 days), Pylon (10 plays in 2 weeks), CandorIQ (onboarded in 12 days), Juicebox ("a couple weeks by myself"), HyperComply (CISO reply soon after first sequence).
6. **Focus and prioritisation rather than more volume.** 6 stories: Peridio ("helped us decide where to focus"), Perplexity, Juicebox (Fortune 500 vs individual sign-up), Campfire (exclusions, warmest inboxes), HyperComply ("the right ones"), Navattic (know when to act and when to let the system handle it).
7. **Emails actually land (managed deliverability).** 7 stories: Spellbook (same copy 70 to 80% vs 19 to 25%), IEG ("fixed that almost overnight"), CandorIQ (bounce 15% to under 2%), Justworks (>10% bounces prevented), Together AI, Anrok, Guru.
8. **Brand-safe personalisation they own.** 8 stories: Perplexity (usage quoted back), Justworks, IEG (ESG research in email), Together AI (package by role), HyperComply, Peridio (CEO voice), Juicebox (enterprise messaging), Spellbook ("ownership of your messaging and protects your brand in a way outsourced campaigns never can").

### 1.3 What is unique in how Unify delivers results

- **"Plays" as the unit of work:** a saved rule of signal, audience filter (including CRM ownership and exclusions), persona contact discovery, enrichment, AI-written sequence and follow-up logic, measured by pipeline per play ([Justworks](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/justworks), [Campfire](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/campfire), [Navattic](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/navattic)). Customers think and report in plays (Guru runs 96).
- **Owned sending infrastructure:** managed domains, mailboxes, warming and bounce prevention are part of the product, which is why the same copy performs better ([Spellbook](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/spellbook), [Innovate Energy Group](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/innovate-energy-group)).
- **Done-with-you onboarding:** product growth strategists and Professional Services translate the customer's ICP and founder messaging into a starter play library ([Guru](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/guru), [Peridio](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/peridio), [Innovate Energy Group](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/innovate-energy-group)).
- **Custom research agents for niche signals:** an agent reads public company pages for a bespoke need (ESG and energy-cost plans; sales-tax exposure) and writes it into the message ([Innovate Energy Group](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/innovate-energy-group), [Anrok](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/anrok)).
- **Business context captured once, reused by chat:** company URL gives ICP, personas and value props, so lists and sequences need no re-prompting ([CandorIQ](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/candoriq), [Juicebox](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/juicebox)).

### 1.4 What Demand AI can adopt from Unify

- **Prospecting and Qualified lead identification:** build a "public commitment" research agent like IEG's: scan target companies' sites, annual reports and press for plant expansions, capex plans, energy or emissions targets, then attach the finding as the qualification reason ([Innovate Energy Group](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/innovate-energy-group)).
- **Segmentation and NBA and NBC:** package outreach as Plays (trigger, CRM ownership gate, exclusions, persona set, sequence, escalation to other contacts after silence) and report pipeline per play ([Justworks](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/justworks), [Navattic](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/navattic), [Campfire](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/campfire)). Inference: for 20 to 40 touch cycles, exclusions and contact-fatigue limits are as important as the trigger.
- **Follow-up and response tracking:** re-open closed-lost deals on a dated rule (for example 6 months, or next budget cycle) and on a "what changed" event (new product, new certification, price change) ([HyperComply](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/hypercomply), [Guru](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/guru), [Spellbook](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/spellbook)).
- **Campaign execution:** trade-show plays: upload attendee or exhibitor lists, map to target accounts and personas, contact before the show ([Juicebox](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/juicebox), [Peridio](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/peridio)). Include managed deliverability so volume does not damage the client's domain ([Spellbook](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/spellbook)).
- **Content creation:** write in the founder's or senior engineer's voice and cite the trigger in the first line; offer a side-by-side quality check against reps' own research before automating ([Peridio](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/peridio), [Abacum](https://www.unifygtm.com/customers/abacum)).
